# P1sowifi: Alterantive Internet

P1sowifi by Low Bandwidth Dreams

P1sowifi is an exploration of a peer-to-peer, decentralized, alternative internet tailored to the context of an archipelagic third world country like the Philippines.

The internet has provided reliable and accessible forms of communication, allowing us to connect instantly with friends, loved ones, and colleagues across continents at near light speed. Despite these advancements, Big Tech has weaponized the network—harvesting personal data to target individual netizens. At the same time, geopolitical divides, conflict, and economic sanctions raise questions about how long physical internet infrastructure can endure. Meanwhile, mega-corporations like Starlink and its competitors are attempting to monopolize global infrastructure through satellite networks.

P1sowifi envisions a fragmented world where future internet infrastructure is either destroyed or completely compromised. The project experiments with low-cost, low-power methods to store data and transmit messages across distances.

P1sowifi Node
Each p1sowifi node is a tiny, solar-powered device capable of connecting to neighboring nodes—the more nodes in the grid, the greater the network's range. For the initial iteration, the goal is to construct the physical node, transmit short messages, and display lightweight net art.
